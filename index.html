06 / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get into the detail.

Do you calculate or file my taxes everywhere?

No. The core offering is transaction reconciliation and accountant-ready data. Tax preparation, filing, consulting, financial reporting and payroll depend on confirmed availability and appropriately qualified professionals in your country. No authorization to file in all listed countries is claimed.

Do I need to connect my wallet?

No. This website does not connect wallets, request private keys or seed phrases, or accept document uploads. Never share signing credentials. Any future document exchange must use a separately agreed secure process.

What if my transaction history is incomplete?

The proposed work can include reconstructing available history, identifying duplicates and documenting missing information. Some gaps may remain; assumptions and unresolved items should be recorded for accountant review.

Can you help with DeFi, NFTs or mining?

Yes, the proposed scope includes organizing records for DeFi, NFTs, staking, mining and airdrops. Tax treatment needs a jurisdiction-specific assessment. We provide no investment recommendations.

Are the prices final?

No. All displayed prices are illustrative, preliminary USD starting points. The written scope, final fees, any applicable taxes, service availability and professional qualifications must be agreed before any engagement.

Can I send an inquiry through this site?

Not yet. There is currently no connected submission service. The form does not transmit or persist your details, and submitting it shows an error.
